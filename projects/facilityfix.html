THE STORY

The problem

When people report a campus issue, status and ownership can become unclear.

The approach

FacilityFix frames reporting as a workflow: capture the issue, clarify ownership and track progress.

Key ideas & features

  • Structure issue reports clearly.
  • Support reporting and status changes.
  • Think through roles, risks and module interactions.

What I learned

Useful applications need workflows, roles and thoughtful edge-case handling.
