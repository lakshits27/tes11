THE STORY

The problem

Habit apps can become cluttered or demanding. A useful tracker should make it easy to return and keep moving.

The approach

Veylo focuses on personal routines, a calm interface and clear interactions.

Key ideas & features

  • Track habits and return to a daily routine.
  • Make consistency easier to review.
  • Keep navigation focused on mobile.

What I learned

Small interaction details matter in apps people use every day.
