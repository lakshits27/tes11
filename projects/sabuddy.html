THE STORY

The problem

Creator ideas often live across notes, drafts and half-written captions.

The approach

SaBuddy focuses on bringing ideas, scripts, captions and publishing plans closer together.

Key ideas & features

  • Organise social content ideas.
  • Keep captions and scripts close to each idea.
  • Plan content ahead instead of relying on memory.

What I learned

AI should remove friction while leaving creators in control of their voice.
