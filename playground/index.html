Interactive lab / 004

Where the code gets to play.

Two original visual experiments: a procedural prism field you can bend and pulse, and a small orbit system you can compose. No external libraries, no heavy 3D engine — just Canvas, SVG, CSS, and TypeScript.

Canvas + SVG Keyboard friendly Reduced-motion aware

Experiment 01 / Procedural light

Prism field.

Layered spectral waves respond to controls and pointer energy. Drag across the canvas to bend the field, or send a pulse through every layer.

LIVE CANVAS
An animated field of layered rainbow wave lines. Use the controls to change amplitude, dispersion, and speed.
