02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

One person.
Many rabbit holes.

I'm not trying to fit into one box. The fun is in connecting different worlds.

I'm a Computer Science undergrad who learns by building. I like taking a rough idea, figuring out how it should work, then refining it until it feels thoughtful and useful.

I care about interfaces that feel intentional, honest claims, and practical details people notice when software is actually in their hands. Still learning, still breaking things, still shipping improvements.

And when I'm away from the editor? Usually music, games, space, futuristic ideas or a completely unrelated question that somehow becomes a new project.
