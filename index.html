02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Not just one thing.

I like connecting dots between fields that don't always seem related.

I'm a Computer Science undergrad who likes making ideas real — from small utilities that remove annoying friction to apps that help people organise their lives.

I'm learning by building, breaking things, and figuring out why they broke. I care about the details: thoughtful interfaces, honest product claims, and software that feels good to use instead of merely existing.

Outside code, I'm into music, games, space, futuristic ideas and the feeling of discovering something new. The long-term plan? Keep getting better, make work I'm proud of, and stay curious enough to surprise myself.
